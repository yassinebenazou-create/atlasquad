## 6. Internal Links Added

- The homepage now links descriptive activity phrases to the 1-hour quad, camel ride, hot air balloon and paragliding detail pages.
- Every tour detail page links to three relevant Marrakech activities using descriptive tour titles.
- Existing tour cards, deal cards, navigation and breadcrumbs continue to link to clean canonical tour URLs.
- Breadcrumb structured data connects Home, Marrakech Tours and each tour detail page.

## 7. Pages That Need More Verified Content

- **1-hour quad:** the strongest current page; verified cancellation terms would still be useful if the business provides them.
- **2-hour quad:** needs confirmed route details, inclusions, transport, safety requirements, price and cancellation terms.
- **Quad and camel:** needs the confirmed time split between activities, route, inclusions, transport, price and safety details.
- **Camel ride:** needs confirmed inclusions, meeting or pickup details, recommended clothing, price and cancellation terms.
- **Hot air balloon:** needs one confirmed duration and price. Existing project data currently conflicts: the featured card says 3 hours and EUR70, while the listing says 20 minutes and EUR140; the detail page currently asks visitors to contact the team for price.
- **Paragliding:** needs the confirmed launch area, provider, participant requirements, transport details, weather policy, price and cancellation terms.
- No unverified policies, availability claims, review counts or cancellation promises were added.

## 8. Suggested Future SEO Landing Pages

Create these only after enough verified, category-level content is available:

- `/quad-marrakech/` for comparing the 1-hour and 2-hour quad options.
- `/camel-ride-marrakech/` for camel-only and quad-plus-camel choices.
- `/hot-air-balloon-marrakech/` when flight timing, transfer and operational details are confirmed.
- `/paragliding-marrakech/` when provider, location and participant requirements are confirmed.
- `/things-to-do-marrakech/` only if it becomes a genuinely useful activity guide rather than a duplicate tours page.

Do not create both `/quad-marrakech/` and `/quad-palmeraie-marrakech/` unless each page can serve a clearly distinct intent with unique content.
